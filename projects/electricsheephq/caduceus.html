overview

Caduceus is a Hermes plugin that combines DNA data, health information and research in a SQLite vault on the user's machine. It provides cited health answers, rebuilds Promethease reports, creates a brief for a clinician and can watch for new research on the user's gene–drug pairs.

  • Imports DNA, Apple Health and health-record files.
  • Keeps the vault and imports on the user's machine.
  • Includes research-watch and clinician-brief features.

Caduceus: Personalized Health & DNA AI

Health answers that cite your own DNA, Apple Health and records, plus new research, from a vault on your machine.

▶ Watch the demo on X

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3a4fc6dc-913e-4c0b-8b0d-a36032022330

Information with limits, not medical advice. Caduceus never tells you to start, stop or change a medication.

Install

hermes plugins install electricsheephq/caduceus --enable

It needs Hermes Agent 0.23.0 or newer and nothing else: no API keys, no extra packages. Restart Hermes, then try the demo below. To use your own files, tell your agent "set up Caduceus". The setup skill takes you through:

  1. importing your files;
  2. recording your medications;
  3. choosing a privacy level;
  4. turning on the research watch.

Once your files are in, ask "walk me through my results". The session skill goes one area at a time, asks before it shows any sensitive result, and ends with a brief for your clinician.

Caduceus is also in the Hermes plugin catalog. Installing it by name (hermes plugins install caduceus --enable) needs a Hermes that can read the current catalog (hermes-agent#151856); the line above always works. Without --enable, Hermes installs the plugin switched off; hermes plugins enable caduceus turns it on.

Where each part runs

Where What you use
Hermes chat (CLI or Desktop; messaging apps are not tested yet) The commands you type: /caduceus help, /caduceus demo, /caduceus privacy …, /caduceus erase, and the watches /blueprint caduceus:research-watch and /blueprint caduceus:daily-check
Your agent The tools caduceus_capture, caduceus_genome, caduceus_trend and caduceus_search. Ask in plain words and the agent calls them
A shell For Caduceus, only the hermes plugins … commands: install, enable and list (for example hermes plugins install electricsheephq/caduceus --enable). There is no hermes caduceus shell command

Try it

  1. Install with the line above and restart Hermes.

  2. Type /caduceus demo. Caduceus writes three files for an invented person and imports them in about a minute:

    • a DNA raw-data file;
    • a variant report;
    • an Apple Health export with health records.

    The demo loads only into an empty vault, so it never mixes with your own data.

  3. Ask "Why isn't my Ozempic working?". One answer comes back, and each part names its source:

    • Apple Health: weight fell about 0.3 kg a week for three months after the recorded semaglutide start, then levelled off.
    • Health records: HbA1c went from 8.4% to 7.8%, still above the reference range.
    • Genome: one marker linked to how people respond to this kind of drug is flagged. Its evidence is small and mixed: one 2023 study found an effect, and a 2026 study found none. The answer also cites a 2026 Nature study on genetics and weight loss with this drug class.
    • Next step: bring these numbers to the prescriber.

    Then try "Would Mounjaro be different for me?" and "Am I losing muscle on Ozempic?".

  4. When you are done, /caduceus erase shows what would be deleted. /caduceus erase confirm then deletes everything Caduceus stored on this machine, the demo included.

What you get

Caduceus brings these into one SQLite vault on your own machine:

  • your 23andMe, AncestryDNA, MyHeritage, FamilyTreeDNA or Living DNA raw data;
  • your Promethease report;
  • your Apple Health export;
  • your US health records.

What that gives you:

  • One answer from all your data: your genotype, your weight curve, your lab results and published research, each cited.
  • A GLP-1 companion: lean mass, protein and waist from Apple Health sit beside your weight, so "Am I losing muscle on Ozempic?" gets your own numbers before and after the start date.
  • Your vault stays on your machine: search, imports and reports run locally. The agent reads only the slices a question needs.
  • Your variant report, rebuilt offline: your Promethease report becomes a self-contained page in Caduceus' own design. Caduceus also writes a one-page health brief with charts to bring to your doctor.
  • It keeps watching new research about your genes and medications: studies on your own gene–drug pairs come first, and a digest arrives only when something new appears.

The screenshots use the synthetic demo persona, not a real person's data.

How it compares

Stock Hermes (attached files and memory) Caduceus
A DNA raw-data file (600,000 lines or more) Too big to read whole; the agent sees slices of raw text Imported once; any marker is looked up in milliseconds
An Apple Health export Too big to read whole Daily trends with units and coverage gaps
Where your data lives Chat attachments and memory notes One SQLite vault on your machine
Gene–drug checks The model's general knowledge CPIC guidance plus GLP-1 research, each cited with its evidence level
Before and after a medication start No A trend comparison around the start date
New research No A research watch on your own gene–drug pairs

Other health plugins in the Hermes catalog read Apple Health, records or medications. When Caduceus launched in November 2026, none of them read DNA raw data or watched research.

What it reads

File How to get it
23andMe raw data (v3, v4, v5), .zip or .txt Request your raw data download in your 23andMe settings
AncestryDNA raw data, .zip Download your DNA data in your AncestryDNA settings
MyHeritage raw data, .zip or .csv Download your raw DNA data in your MyHeritage DNA settings
FamilyTreeDNA Family Finder raw data, .csv.gz Family Finder → download raw data (the build 37 file)
Living DNA raw data, .zip or .txt Download your raw data in your Living DNA settings
Promethease report, .zip or .html The report file you downloaded from Promethease
Apple Health export: the .zip from an iPhone in any language, or the export XML inside it iPhone Health app → your picture → Export All Health Data. With Health Records connected, the zip includes your clinical records
Health Auto Export JSON (or a zip of JSON exports) In Health Auto Export, use Manual Export or an automation that saves metrics as JSON
FHIR R4 JSON (labs, conditions, medications) A patient portal's FHIR or "download my record" export

Give your agent the file's path, and Caduceus copies the file into its vault and imports it.

  • The agent is told never to open these files itself.
  • Big imports keep running in the background, and re-importing a file does nothing.
  • An interrupted import resumes where it stopped.
  • A FHIR condition that gives only the date it was recorded is kept, with its onset unknown. Results without a code are told apart by name.
  • A vault holds one person. A second DNA file is compared with the first on shared markers, and if it looks like a different person, you are told, and answers carry a warning. Keep each person in their own Hermes profile.

Health Auto Export

For a JSON export, use Manual Export in Health Auto Export (HAE), or an automation that saves JSON files to a folder, then import the file with caduceus_capture op=import. .hae AutoSync files are HAE's private format and cannot be imported.

For live sync, run the HAE app's MCP server and configure it in Hermes. The operator allows its server name in config.yaml (use your configured name if different):

plugins:
  entries:
    caduceus:
      mcp_allowlist: [health-auto-export]

Then call caduceus_capture op=sync. /blueprint caduceus:hae-sync keeps it current daily and stays silent unless a sync fails, including one that failed in the background after its call returned. The hae_mcp_server setting defaults to health-auto-export. If your server has another name, put that name in the allowlist and set hae_mcp_server to it too: the allowlist grants access, and the setting picks the server. hae_backfill_days defaults to 90 for the first sync, and a first sync that fails or is interrupted keeps that start for the next try. Later syncs start two days before the last day read in full. A day whose reply was too large is read again by the next sync. A sync in which no reply could be read fails and says so, and the next one reads those days again. When the server answers with an error, Caduceus reports a fixed message and never passes on the server's own text. The sync asks for whole days in the phone's own time zone. If no entry of one kind (health metrics, sleep or workouts) can be read, the sync fails, says which kind, and the next one reads those days again. If a file holds a night's sleep summary and that night's stages from the same source, the summary is used, so the night counts once.

Tools

Tool What it does
caduceus_search Full-text search across your annotations, research, labs, conditions, medications and notes, with cited snippets; lists everything of one kind, such as your medications
caduceus_genome Your genotype at any marker, gene views (every marker Caduceus knows for the gene, its derived call first), gene–drug checks (CPIC guidance plus GLP-1 research), derived calls (APOE, CYP2C19, CYP2C9 and others), a gene–drug profile, top findings, and the rebuilt variant report
caduceus_trend Apple Health trends with units, coverage gaps and a calculation version; before and after a medication start; lab results over time; a daily signal check
caduceus_capture Imports and their progress, medications (recorded only after you confirm), notes, the research watch, settings, and the health brief

A gene–drug result keeps three outcomes apart:

  • flag: you carry the effect allele;
  • no flag: it was tested and is absent;
  • not assessed: not on your chip, or a no-call.

"Not assessed" is never reported as normal.

Privacy

Level What shows
open (default) Everything you imported, except categories you hide
standard Alzheimer's risk (APOE), hereditary cancer risk, carrier status, mental health and reproductive results stay hidden until you unlock each one

You change these yourself, with /caduceus privacy standard, /caduceus hide <category>, /caduceus unlock <category> and so on (/caduceus help lists them), or in the plugin settings. Your agent can read the settings but cannot change them. Hidden results are counted, never described. A marker follows its gene's category: hiding pgx hides the drug-response genes' markers in gene views, lookups, gene–drug checks and the summary's gene–drug profile, and a derived call shows only when every marker behind it can. Variant-report listings (filters, top findings, the rebuilt report) follow each annotation's own category.

Disclosure — imports, search and reports run locally, and the vault is never uploaded as a whole; tool results (slices of your data) enter the conversation, so the model provider you chose sees them; a hook adds one fixed note, holding none of your data, to the first health, medication or DNA question of each chat when the vault has data (off with the vault_hint setting); the research watch (on by default, runs when you or the agent ask for it or on a schedule you create, off with the research_watch setting) sends gene and topic terms, the names of medications you recorded, and public marker and article IDs, never genotypes, to Europe PMC and NCBI; the Health Auto Export sync (off until you allow that app's MCP server for Caduceus in Hermes config, and run only when you or the agent ask for it or on a schedule you create) reads Apple Health data from that server through Hermes's own MCP client and sends none of your data out; scheduled checks run model turns billed like any message; no telemetry. Includes a report-a-problem skill: the agent drafts a bug report, asks you to check it holds no health data, and files it to this repository with gh only after you approve the draft.

To delete everything Caduceus stored, run /caduceus erase: it shows what would go, and /caduceus erase confirm deletes it. Your settings stay, and so do schedules you created with /blueprint: the preview names each one and what its next run will do.

Your data lives in your Hermes profile's plugin-data/caduceus/. In v0.1 it is not encrypted at rest; encrypted and local-model levels are planned for v0.4. See SECURITY.md for the boundaries.

Settings

Setting Default What it does
privacy_level open open or standard (see Privacy above)
hidden_categories none Categories never shown at any level
unlocked_categories none Sensitive categories you chose to see at standard
research_watch on Lets research-watch runs query Europe PMC and NCBI with gene, drug and topic terms. Genotypes are never sent
research_topics none Topics to watch besides your genes and medications
vault_hint on Adds the short fixed note to the first health question of each chat (see Accuracy and limits)

Change them in the plugin settings, or for privacy with /caduceus commands.

Research watch and daily check

  • /blueprint caduceus:research-watch:
    • What it does: a digest of new studies (Europe PMC, LitVar2) and ClinVar changes about your genes, medications and chosen topics, weekly by default.
    • First run: 3 studies to start with. Studies on your own gene–drug pairs come first (for example a gene you carry and a medicine you take, from the last five years), then papers that mention your markers, then general results. Each item says why it was picked.
    • After that: only new ones, in the same order, and nothing when there is nothing new.
    • What it sends: gene and topic words, the names of medications you recorded, marker IDs from the built-in knowledge pack, and article IDs. Which terms it sends depends only on your medications and topics, never on your genotypes.
    • When it runs: when you or your agent ask for it, or on the schedule the blueprint creates. It is on by default; the research_watch setting turns it off.
  • /blueprint caduceus:daily-check: once a day, checks your Apple Health trends (resting heart rate, sleep, weight, steps) and messages you only when something is outside its threshold. It costs one model call a day.

Accuracy and limits

  • Chips miss things. Consumer DNA chips test common variants. Rare variants can be missed or miscalled: in one large study, most rare pathogenic chip calls did not confirm. Anything important needs a clinical test.
  • Some genes can't be read from chips. CYP2D6, HLA types and copy-number changes are reported as not assessed. An ambiguous APOE pattern stays ambiguous.
  • Small effects, labelled evidence. Genetic effects on drug response are usually small. Every result carries its evidence level (a CPIC guideline, or research association), its sources and its limits.
  • No medical advice. Caduceus never tells you to start, stop or change a medication. Talk to your prescriber.
  • A reminder for every model. The first health, medication or DNA question in each chat carries one short fixed note for the model: answer from your vault's tools, and leave medication decisions to your prescriber. It keeps smaller models from answering from general knowledge. The note holds none of your data; the vault_hint setting turns it off.

Data sources and licences

  • CPIC: gene–drug guidance data, CC0.
  • NCBI: ClinVar, dbSNP and LitVar2 (public).
  • Europe PMC: article metadata.
  • SNPedia: its text (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0) appears only from your own Promethease report. Caduceus keeps it on your machine, labels it, and never ships or fetches it.

Development

Python 3.11+, standard library only.

python -m pip install pytest
python -m pytest

Tests use synthetic data generated at test time and never touch the network. Module contracts are in docs/contracts.md, agent guidance in AGENTS.md, and how to contribute in CONTRIBUTING.md.

Troubleshooting

Problem What to do
hermes plugins install caduceus can't find it Use hermes plugins install electricsheephq/caduceus --enable
The caduceus_* tools don't show up Run hermes plugins enable caduceus, then restart Hermes
An import seems stuck Big Apple Health exports take a few minutes and keep running in the background. Ask your agent for the import status
"That path cannot be read" Caduceus refuses credential files and other profiles' secrets. Give the path of the export itself
/caduceus demo refuses to load It loads only into an empty vault. Try it in a fresh Hermes profile
The agent answers without citing your data Keep the vault_hint setting on, or say "use Caduceus" in your question

Report a bug

  • Bugs: use the bug form. Your agent can draft it: ask it to "report a problem with Caduceus". Never put health data in a report; describe the problem with the demo persona's data instead.
  • Security or privacy problems: report them privately (see SECURITY.md).
