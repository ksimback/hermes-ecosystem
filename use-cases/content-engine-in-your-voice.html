why this stack

The stories that produce content people actually ship all separate three things that beginners collapse into one prompt: what to write about, how you sound, and whether it reads as machine-written. Memory carries your voice, because voice is a corpus problem and not a prompt problem. The research skills feed the topic pipeline. The production plugin and the de-slop pass are the quality gate, and they are the reason the output is publishable rather than merely generated. Automated publishing goes last, deliberately — it is the step you should trust least early on.

caveats
  • Automated posting is publishing. Keep a human approval step until the voice is genuinely right; a bad post is not retractable.
  • Platform automation depends on third-party API access that changes without warning — expect maintenance on the publishing layer specifically.
